# Storytelling

## Headlines

- A headline is a full claim with a verb: "Возвраты упали вдвое после
  самопроверки", not "Качество".
- Lead with the answer (pyramid principle): conclusion first, evidence after.
- Quantify when you can: "+38% активных команд" beats "заметный рост".
- 12 words or fewer. Cut adjectives first, then hedges ("достаточно",
  "в целом", "некоторые").
- The eyebrow names the topic. The headline makes the point.

Test: read only the headlines in order. If that read-through tells the whole
story, the deck works.

## Russian typography

- Use the minus sign `−` for negatives (−35%), the en dash `–` for ranges
  (3–5), and the em dash ` — ` with spaces in sentences.
- Use guillemets «ёлочки» for quotes in text (the quote layout adds its own
  mark).
- Group thousands with a space: 1 240, 12 500 ₽.
- Keep units short: ч, мин, тыс., млн, млрд. Put no period after "млн" in a
  stat value.

## Structures

### Review (quarterly, project, retro), 8–12 slides
1. `title`
2. `statement`: the one-line verdict
3. `bento`: the period at a glance
4. `section`: what worked
5. 1–2 slides of evidence (`split` with a chart, or `stats`)
6. `section`: what didn't
7. `bullets` or `split`: causes and fixes
8. `quote`: a user or stakeholder voice
9. `timeline`: next steps
10. `statement`: the ask or decision needed

### Pitch / proposal, 10–14 slides
1. `title`
2. `statement`: the problem, in the audience's words
3. `stats`: the cost of the problem
4. `split` with an image or stat: the solution
5. `bento`: how it works or key features
6. `compare`: options, with ours highlighted
7. `stats` or a `split` chart: traction or proof
8. `quote`: a customer
9. `timeline`: the plan
10. `statement`: the ask (money, approval, people)

### Research / analysis readout, 8–12 slides
1. `title`
2. `statement`: the key finding
3. `bullets`: the 3 questions we asked
4. One `split` per finding (chart plus a 1-line claim)
5. `bento`: summary of findings
6. `compare` or `bullets`: implications
7. `statement`: the recommendation

### Status update, 4–6 slides
`title` → `stats` (health) → `timeline` (progress) → `bullets` (risks) →
`statement` (help needed).

## Notes

Put everything you would *say* but not *show* into `notes`: methodology,
caveats, sources, full numbers. A sparse slide with rich notes beats a dense
slide.
